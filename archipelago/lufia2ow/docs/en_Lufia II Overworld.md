# Lufia II Overworld

## What does randomization do to this game?

The contents of the 171 red chests are shuffled. For now, tools and keys stay in their vanilla chests, so the story
plays in its usual order.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Daos.

## What does another world's item look like?

An "AP item" key item. The chest is marked opened and the client sends the item to its owner.
