# Lufia II Overworld

## What does randomization do to this game?

The contents of the red chests and of most story rewards are shuffled across the multiworld, tools and keys
included. The story keeps its usual order; the logic follows it, so every key and tool can be reached before the
dungeon that needs it.

The options choose how far keys and tools travel (anywhere, your own world, their own story stage or their vanilla
spot), whether story rewards keep their own item, and whether the other items are the vanilla ones or random ones.

The Ship, the Submarine and the airship can also be found as items, as three Progressive Ships or as a Ship, a
Submarine and an Engine; the story still gives them too. The Arrow and Fire arrow can be progressive; Maxim always
starts with the Arrow, as in vanilla.

Some townspeople tell you where one of your progression items is, in your world or in another player's. Talking to
them can also unlock that hint on the server.

## What is the goal?

Beat the game by default: defeat Daos (the goal counts before the ending plays). The Goal option can end the game
earlier instead: opening the Shrine of Daos, the destruction of Chaed, Amon in the Divine Shrine, Idura in the Tower of
Sacrifice or Gades in the Ancient Tower. Locations after the goal are left out.

The Dragon eggs goal: bring 8 Dragon eggs to the Egg Dragon's shrine per wish, and make as many wishes as Egg Wishes
asks (1 to 4). The multiworld holds 8 eggs per wish; one of them is the egg Merix gives you.

## What does another world's item look like?

An "AP item" key item. The client reports the check and the server sends the item to its owner.
