# Lufia II Overworld

## What does randomization do to this game?

The contents of the red chests and of most story rewards are shuffled across the multiworld, tools and keys
included. The story keeps its usual order; the logic follows it, so every key and tool can be reached before the
dungeon that needs it.

The options choose how far keys and tools travel (anywhere, your own world, their own story stage or their vanilla
spot), whether story rewards keep their own item, and whether the other items are the vanilla ones or random ones.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Daos by default. The Goal option can end the game earlier instead: opening the Shrine of Daos, the destruction
of Chaed, Amon in the Divine Shrine, Idura in the Tower of Sacrifice or Gades in the Ancient Tower. Locations after
the goal are left out.

## What does another world's item look like?

An "AP item" key item. The client reports the check and the server sends the item to its owner.
